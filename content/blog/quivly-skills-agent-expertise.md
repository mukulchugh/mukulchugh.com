An agent can write a polished QBR and still miss the churn signal sitting in plain sight. Usually the model isn't dumb — it just doesn't have the workflow judgment your best CS lead carries around.

[Quivly Skills](https://github.com/quivly/skills) packages that expertise for customer engineering and post-sales: health reviews, churn checks, QBR prep, the work after the deal closes.

A prompt in a tab dies when the tab closes. A skill can travel across tools and stay versioned. The bar we use: specific to a real job, operational enough to review, portable enough that we're not maintaining five slightly different copies.

Fork it. Argue with it. Send a PR when your team has a sharper check than we do.
