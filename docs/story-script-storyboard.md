# The screen stayed on

Script and shot storyboard, draft 1. 28 September 2026.

**For Mukul's review.** This document proposes 34 passages for the dedicated `/story` page. It contains the complete visible script, expanded reading copy, character actions, desktop and portrait compositions, transitions and an art-proof brief. It is a written storyboard, not rendered frames or a validated animation. Nothing here changes the live prototype.

The current build remains **39 beats, 12 chapters, 23 selectable years, 237,100ms of playback and an 18-second ending**. Its technical fix commit is `f63f96c`. Those values remain authoritative until an approved script is implemented and measured.

## 1. The story we are telling

A father shows his four-year-old son how to use the internet. The boy keeps exploring. When the computer breaks, another person shows him what is inside it. He begins writing and building, finds people who want his work, and learns to organise experiences they can share. The rooms and responsibilities grow. Eventually, he is the person sitting beside someone else, helping them understand.

The present-day ending stays small: Mukul working, absorbed in something unfinished. **Still building.** After the work and contact links, the last line returns to the person who began it: **It started with my dad.**

The change is from receiving help to making useful things and sharing what he learns. That interpretation connects confirmed memories; it supplies no invented hardship, conversation or career motivation.

Three movements, with twelve navigation chapters retained:

| Movement | Passages | What changes | Physical rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Someone shows me | 01–06 | A computer becomes a place to explore; people become teachers. | Sky → close room → workshop → connected screens. |
| I make things for people | 07–23 | Publishing becomes paid work, collaboration, physical events and products. | Intimate making opens into a town, campus and shared spaces. |
| I pass it on | 24–34 | Team responsibility, explaining, mentoring and building from the beginning again. | A long departure, inhabited workspaces, then a quiet close. |

The career chapters still have facts and roles. They do not need invented revenue or a manufactured crisis to carry weight. Mukul's new Quivly memory makes that turn specific: the opportunity to be a founding engineer and generalist in an AI-native, San Francisco-based company. YC backing belongs to its later 2026 development, not his reason for joining in 2025.

## 3. Direction shared by every passage

**A connected world.** Rooms share a continuous spatial route, with thresholds, floors, windows and near-field objects carrying transitions. Desktop travels sideways with selected inward and outward arcs. Portrait travels vertically through separately composed stages. Adjacent eras are connected artistically, not presented as literal neighbouring buildings. No blank journey with only a floating cable.

**People act.** Each passage has a leading verb. Seated characters shift attention, reach, explain or test something. A new logo on an unchanged screen is not a new scene. Poses are authored and scrubbed from progress; idle loops cannot continue after Pause.

**Three scales.** Close for a lesson or decision; medium for shared work; wide for physical events and a change of city. Stay on the side of the interaction that keeps faces and hands visible. Large camera arcs belong to departures, not every title. The lens never passes through a monitor, desk, head or aircraft shell: portal transitions use a designed opening and a continuous view between two real sets.

**Copy has a reserved place.** Syne headings and Geist body retain the existing portfolio identity. Desktop copy stays in the right reading region; people and essential artifacts occupy the left and centre. Portrait puts readable copy above the action, with the dock below. Test the longest titles and body copy before fitting the camera. Never shrink a whole room to make a desktop shot fit a phone. Short landscape retains its bounded, scrollable reading region above the dock.

**Colour belongs to places.** Cloud grey and warm daylight → amber workshop → off-white paper and screen light → campus daylight → cabin blue → cooler shared work → warm final light. Terracotta is a restrained thread in materials, wayfinding and controls. No green Zero identity, new display font, top-right name label, bloom haze or glowing particles used to disguise empty staging.

**Continuity motif.** A hand points, the learner looks, then acts. Dad introduces it in 01, Mandal repeats it in 04–05, colleagues exchange it in 25, and Mukul gives it back in 27. Local cables remain grounded props. Do not invent one physically unchanged cable running from 2004 into 2026.

**Artifacts are evidence, not wallpaper.** Real pages stay inside their era's display or a deliberate inspection surface. The source opens through the existing HTML control. A public logo is not an app screenshot. Use the seven already inspected captures; additional captures require inspection and provenance before entering the build. No generated private chats, bills, analytics, client data or plausible-looking historic receipts.

**Memory is not a photograph.** All proposed hand gestures, clothing, room layouts, routes and composite people below are illustrative staging. They are not newly claimed recollections. One visible laptop maximum in a composition, including transitions. Phones remain handheld scale. Unknown hardware purchase dates and monitor arrangement stay unknown.

### 01 · My dad opened my world.

**Caption:** 2004 · Rudrapur, Uttarakhand

**Body:** I was four. My dad sat beside me at our white computer and showed me how to Google.

**Read:** A white monitor, a TVS keyboard and a computer cabinet. That was the beginning. My dad showed me how to use the internet, and I kept coming back to it.

- **Action and turn:** Dad points; the child follows his hand to the screen, then reaches for the mouse. The aerial town becomes one particular shared moment.
- **Desktop shot:** Begin among layered clouds above a restrained low-rise town. Descend along an open side of a cutaway room, then settle at child eye height in a three-quarter two-shot. Dad, child, hand and white CRT remain left of the copy. The handoff, not the building reveal, is the held composition.
- **Portrait shot:** Cloud depth travels down into the room beneath the text. Dad leans beside the child so both faces and the keyboard fit the lower stage. Do not place one head behind the other.
- **Exit:** Push toward the CRT after the gesture lands, retaining its bezel and the child's silhouette. A visible opening leads into the next activity. No cut to an anonymous screen.
- **Artifact / boundary:** Illustrative white computer and simple search surface; no claim to reproduce the exact 2004 Google page. Preserve the original memory wording in the source record.
