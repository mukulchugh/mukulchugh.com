## Decision and verification boundaries

Recommend C for personal range, A for editorial simplicity, B when browsing is the primary goal. Wait for user selection before implementation. Keep large editorial covers, Syne/Geist roles, real uniform borders, existing neutral/lime palette, accessible links, and reduced-motion support.

Images are concept illustrations. Generated dates and descriptive copy are not verified factual content; implementation must read canonical post metadata and summaries. Light strips indicate material treatment, not complete light-mode layouts. Mobile behavior is proposed, not yet built or verified for these concepts. The baseline Writing preview and homepage remain unchanged.
