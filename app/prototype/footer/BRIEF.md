# Footer prototype

Approved direction: Concept 5, Compact Index. The owner approved homepage and sitewide integration. The root layout uses components/footer.tsx; this isolated route reuses it. Dock clearance is outside the bordered tile.

Experience mode. Close the portfolio with a personal signature and clear destinations without repeating the contact CTA. Preserve the site's Syne/Geist typography, neutral satin surfaces, 14px corners and real 1px borders.

Desktop: identity, Still building. and copyright on the left; GitHub/Writing in the middle; LinkedIn/X and Back to top on the right. Mobile: identity above two link columns. Writing opens the local blog; social profiles open new tabs with accessible notices.

Motion thesis: a short left-to-right underline confirms the selected destination while its arrow moves 2px outward. No entrance animation, no recurring loops, no layout movement. Keyboard focus has equivalent feedback. Reduced motion retains the underline without spatial movement.
